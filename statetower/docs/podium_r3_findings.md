# ST_Podium: round-3 QC/QA findings (escalated; Engineering and Site Operation accepted 8.5)

- **MAJOR**: The first west bay right of the P8 niche is a wrong-scale miniature bay. POD_Face with epa=2 keeps the 9.6 m grid from P8, so bay 0 is squeezed between the niche jamb (u≈4.84 m) and the first boundary (9.6 m). Its screen is wp = 844-477 = 367 cm, against 728 cm in a standard bay. Because the windows and tracery scale with wp, the bay gets 51 cm windows (102 cm elsewhere), 90 cm sub-arches and a 183 cm head arch: a half-size copy of the bay next to it. Photo 2 contradicts this. In the P8 zoom (ref2 x 355-470), the corner pair and its recess cover x≈368-386 px, the first bay x 386-401 px (15 px) and standard bays about 20 px. So the first bay is about 0.75 of a standard bay, with full-size windows (3 columns × 7 rows), and the first colonnade column sits under x≈398-400 px, about 12-14 m from P8. This is the focal spot of the CAM_DAY and CAM_NIGHT views (portal + P8 corner). The crew left this decision to QC.
  - **FIX:** Start the west bay grid after the niche instead of at P8. (1) In POD_Face, when epa > 1.5: boundary(0) stays at the niche jamb (pier_a - 89). Boundaries i ≥ 1 are X0 + (i-1)*bw2 with X0 ≈ 1200 cm (acceptable range 1100-1300), nb2 = round((L - X0)/bay_t) = 8 and bw2 = (L - X0)/8 ≈ 930 cm. The first bay screen is then about 607 cm and the standard screens about 700 cm. (2) Size ww, wdx, ro_s and ro_o from the standard screen width (wp_std = bw2 - 232), not from each bay's own wp. Drop the first bay to 2 window columns if wp < 600, so no bay gets half-size windows or tracery. (3) Use the same grid for the colonnade cx list in POD_Face (columns at X0 + k*bw2, k = 0..7). Also use it for the POD_LowBlock pts (P8 + wd*(X0 + (i-1)*bww), keeping the LOD1 cylinders and collider boxes aligned) and for POD_FaceLod with epa=2. (4) Re-run the per-part buried lint on T_P8.
- **MAJOR**: The portal's paired giant columns are almost buried in square piers, so the gate tower base reads as four fluted square piers instead of the paired free-standing round columns in photos 2 and 3. In POD_Portal, cpos puts each shaft centre 50 cm inside the face (e.g. (135, D-50)) with r 80, so each shaft stands only 30 cm proud of a 4.88 m-wide pier face (PW = W/2 - AR). The portal-base zoom (iso_back and left, fixed bounds z 0-29 m) shows only faint vertical grooves on solid piers. Photo 2 (zoom ref2 x 280-330, y 697-733) and photo 3 (crop x 165-300) show two fully round white shafts on pedestals at each corner, with dark gaps between and behind them, flanking the giant arch. These columns are the dominant feature of the street view (photo 3) and of the portal base in photo 2, and players see them up close at street level.
  - **FIX:** Make the pairs free-standing while keeping everything within the 50 cm outline. (1) Recess the lower-tier outer faces by 140 cm: north piers y max = D-140 (was D); west and east piers x min = 140 and x max = W-140 on their outer faces. Translate the north copy of spN to y = D-140 and shrink its depth to PD-140. Move the outer copies of spW to x = 140 and W-140. Move the impN/impW imposts, the archivolt + keystone (avk at D-140+11) and the plinths pl back with them. (2) Keep cpos and r 80 unchanged: the shafts then span D-130 to D+30, 10 cm clear of the recessed pier, under the existing rA/rC ressauts. (3) Extend pedN/pedWn/pedWs back to the recessed face (y min ≈ D-150) and raise them to about 320 cm: photo 3 pedestals are about 1/6 of the order. Set the column z0 to the new pedestal top. (4) Apply the same 140 cm recess to the pier boxes in POD_PortalMass detail=1, so the LOD1 8-segment shafts also stand free. The collider is unchanged. (5) Re-lint T_PORTAL.
- **MINOR**: LOD1 lights differently from LOD0 at night. POD_NotchRing kind 3 is a continuous ST_PodiumRooms band (offset -150, zB0 to zB1) around the whole ring. POD_FaceLod adds one tall ST_PodiumRooms panel per bay, about 0.35·bw × 12.7 m. In-engine, interior_window_pbr will show a continuous lit ribbon at 24.8-29.6 m and a bright vertical stripe in every bay. LOD0 and photos 1/2 show a white balustrade band and white screens pierced by small dark or lit windows. LOD1 is likely what renders at the 700 m CAM_NIGHT and CAM_DAY distances.
  - **FIX:** For the kind 3 balcony band, use ST_DarkGlass (or ST_FacadeShade) and add a 70 cm ST_Stone rail band at zB0 to zB0+70 on the same notched ring. In POD_FaceLod, replace each bay's single panel with 3 narrow panels (0.1·bw wide each, centres at ±0.25·wp) or switch them to ST_DarkGlass. Either way the panels must read as window columns in white screens, not one lit stripe.
